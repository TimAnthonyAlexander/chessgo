  opposite of offloading threats.

  #2 small-net gate — the enabling mechanism was already built and measured flat
  (§14.2).

  The gate only pays if you can skip big-net maintenance on lopsided subtrees.
  That is exactly the lazy/deferred accumulator — we implemented it (NNUE_LAZY,
  commit 484685c, bit-identical) and it measured flat-to-slightly-negative: "the
  deferred path's walk-back cost cancels the saved copy on our heavily-pruned /
  high-TT-hit tree." The enriched stack still carries the lazy/dirty[]/ensure()
  scaffolding and a -lean-lazy bench flag — none shipped, for this reason.

  Without maintenance savings, a gate salvages only the forward pass —
  screluDotSIMD 6% flat / accEval ~7% cum — a single-digit ceiling. Against that
  you pay the gate's own per-node cost (material calc + branch + re-eval
  guard). On a backend running at ~95% of the retirement ceiling with optimal 
  codegen (§30, objdump-verified), that's the "trade bookkeeping for downstream
  savings" shape that lost 3/3 in the sweep (batchApply −6%, partition −2.5%,
  staged movegen −2.5%). Expected net ≤ 0.

  Also architecture-specific: our tree evaluates at most nodes (static eval
  feeds RFP/futility/NMP pruning decisions), so few subtrees "never evaluate" —
  the precondition the gate needs is rare here.
