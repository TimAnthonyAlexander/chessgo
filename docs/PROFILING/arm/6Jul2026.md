Eval is ~80% of all engine CPU. It splits into accumulator maintenance (the FT
column add/sub kernels + the king-bucket rebuild) and the forward pass
(`evalFromHalvesLean`, ~9.5%). The int8 FT column add/sub kernels plus their
inlined Load/Store are ~half the entire process. HCE is completely absent — NNUE
is the sole eval. Biggest lever: cut columns-touched-per-node (better deltas,
avoid the 25%-cum king-refresh `buildAccHalf`) or widen those kernels.

> **Caveat (2026-07-07):** the "widen/batch those kernels" half is an **arm64-only**
> lever. The FT int8 kernels look like a fat concentrated ~20%-flat target *here*
> (arm64: weaker vector units vs the M3's huge L1 + HW prefetch), but on prod amd64
> the same kernels are L1-resident at ~5.5–5.9 IPC (near-peak) and batching measures
> **−10–13%** — compute-bound, not memory-bound. Only the **columns-per-move**
> (algorithmic) half transfers to prod. See `docs/ENGINE_STRENGTH.md` §30.3 and
> `docs/PROFILING/amd/6Jul2026.md`.

Delegated NNUE is ~77% (accumulator maintenance 70.6% cum + leaf Evaluate 6.6%
cum), leaving ~20% for real search machinery plus ~3% Lazy-SMP scheduler
overhead. The accumulator-maintenance : leaf-eval ratio is ≈10.8:1 (70.6% vs
6.6%) — the acc refresh on every DoMove dwarfs the leaf forward pass. The
real-search function with the most self-time is `selectMoveLegacy` (4.17% flat),
just ahead of the atomic TT probe (3.98%) and `negamax` self-time (2.98%); SEE,
magic movegen, and the SMP scheduler are all now materially visible. (The earlier
scalar-arm64 run showed ~91% eval / ~5% search — the fast NEON kernels compress
eval's share by ~14 points, making this apples-to-apples with the amd64 SIMD
profiles.)

## Notes

- Both tables are the SIMD arm64/NEON build (`go1.27rc1`, `GOEXPERIMENT=simd`),
  prod KB net `lean.bin` (44 MB), so eval/search ratios are internally consistent.
- This is the arm64/NEON kernel path, not prod amd64/AVX-512 — see
  `docs/PROFILING/amd/6Jul2026.md` for the prod-representative profile. On amd64
  the FT int8 kernels compress harder, so eval drops to ~57% of the process and
  real search (TT probe, ordering) surfaces more; the accumulator path is the
  dominant cost on both arches.
